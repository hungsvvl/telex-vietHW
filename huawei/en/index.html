Install guide

Install G Navigation for your Huawei watch

The phone searches and works out the route; the Huawei watch on your wrist shows the next turn, the distance to it and the time remaining. Everything below is the whole procedure, from download to guidance on the watch.

Package: com.gnavigation.mobile

Four steps

  1. 1

    Install the app on your phone

    Open the G Navigation listing on AppGallery and tap Install. If you are reading this on a computer, scan the code below with your phone camera.

    • Install fromHuawei AppGalleryFor Huawei phones. The watch needs this app before it can receive guidance.
    • Install fromGoogle PlayFor Android phones with Google Play.
  2. 2

    Open it and grant the permissions

    On first run the app asks for Location, Bluetooth and Notifications. Without Location it cannot work out a route; without Bluetooth the watch never receives guidance.

  3. 3

    Connect your watch

    Pair the watch in Huawei Health first and leave that app running in the background. Then open G Navigation, go to the device section and pick your watch; the app installs the navigation screen onto it for you.

  4. 4

    Pick a destination and start

    Search for the address on the phone and tap Start. Turns appear on the watch for the whole ride; keep Bluetooth on and do not swipe the app away.

Supported devices

The guidance screen on your wrist runs on HarmonyOS Lite Wearable 5.0 and later. Every line below has had the HarmonyOS 5 update from Huawei; a watch still on an older build should be updated in Huawei Health before installing.

  • HUAWEI WATCH FIT 3, FIT 4, FIT 4 Pro, FIT 5

    Paired through Huawei Health.

  • HUAWEI WATCH GT 4, GT 5, GT 5 Pro

    Paired through Huawei Health.

Not supported
  • WATCH FIT SE and WATCH FIT 2 - the app mistakes these two screens for a simulator and never connects to the phone.
  • WATCH 4, WATCH 5 and WATCH Ultimate - full HarmonyOS watches; the build for them is not released.
  • WATCH GT 2 and GT 3 - they run LiteOS and take no third-party app.
