02

Data and purposes

  • LocationPrecise or approximate location to show you on the map, find nearby places, calculate routes and guide you, including with the screen off during an active trip.
  • Searches and routesSearch text, start point, destination and route to return results and directions.
  • Notifications (optional)Only when you turn it on: guidance from Google Maps and caller name and call state from Zalo, Messenger, Telegram, Viber, Instagram or LINE, to show them on the watch. With replies from the music panel on: messages that have a reply button (app name, sender, latest text), kept in memory for at most 10 minutes so you can pick one on the watch. Unrelated notifications are ignored; message contents are not stored or uploaded.
  • Contacts (optional)Only when you turn on call alerts: the app creates contacts carrying the caller's name so the watch can show it. For Zalo calls it looks in your contacts for a person with the same name to show that person's number. This happens on the phone; the app does not upload contacts or change or delete existing ones.
  • Voice (optional)Only when you turn on voice replies for a Huawei watch: what you say into the watch's microphone is passed by Huawei Health to G Navigation and turned into text on the phone, which goes back to the watch for you to check and send yourself. With the Vietnamese model (Offline), the audio and the text never leave the phone and are not stored. If you pick Google as the recogniser, the audio is sent to Google's speech recognition service to turn it into text; G Navigation does not store the audio. With the backup replies from the music panel (for watches without a microphone key): only after you answer a reply session on the watch, G Navigation uses the microphone permission to take audio from the watch's microphone over its Bluetooth call channel, recognises it on the phone (or with Google, if you pick it) and sends the reply through the messaging app's reply button. The app does not use the phone's microphone and records nothing.
  • AccountAccount ID, name and email from Google Sign-In and the sign-in session, for sign-in, the trial, Premium and keeping each account on one phone.
  • PurchasesProduct ID and purchase token from Google Play to grant and restore Premium. We never receive card details.
  • Fraud preventionA hashed device identifier, the Google Play Integrity result and a daily hashed IP address, to stop repeated trials.
  • WatchPairing and connection state, to send guidance, maps and calls to the watch you choose.
  • Administrator announcementsFirebase Installation ID and messaging token, to deliver update and service notices.
  • Technical diagnosticsLocal technical logs cover call states, watch maps and the voice path. Manual sending follows the attachment disclosure and your confirmation. Automatic reports for call or map failures are off by default, including earlier automatic-send choices; each channel needs its own affirmative consent. There is no separate automatic voice-error channel, but voice traces may be included in the same report bundle.

G Navigation does not sell data and does not use data for advertising.

04

Data sharing

Data goes only to the parties a feature you use needs, always over HTTPS:

  • Google MapsThe Maps SDK and Google map imagery draw the phone map and the map frames sent to the watch. See the Maps SDK data disclosure.
  • Google Places, Geocoding and RoutesThe primary source for search, place names and routing. Requests travel through the G Navigation server.
  • Guidance voicesThe removal update offers only the G Navigation voice and the phone's system voices. G Navigation uses the Vietnamese VAIS-1000 package (CC BY 4.0; sherpa-onnx) on the phone without sending guidance sentences out. After you agree to download it, the package comes from Cloudflare or GitHub. System voices, including English guidance in other app languages, may send sentences to the engine provider if they are network voices; the provider's settings and policy apply. Earlier installed versions. Versions offering G Navigation 2 (formerly Mẫn Nhi) can still send uncached sentences, which may include road names, to backup routing provider (Google) TTS at backup routing provider and fetch audio from backup routing provider until upgraded. The service receives IP address, voice installation ID, app/Android versions and guest authentication information; TTS requests carry no GPS coordinates. The removal update deletes the old voice selection, consent, audio cache and voice-specific guest credentials. Changing source or this policy does not change an installed app. Version 1.15 and earlier use Google Text-to-Speech through the G Navigation server, sending the sentence without GPS coordinates.
  • Speech modelWith the Vietnamese model (Offline), voice replies are recognised entirely on the phone; the audio is sent to no one. When you choose to download it, the Vietnamese model (Zipformer, Apache 2.0; sherpa-onnx library) is fetched once from the G Navigation server on Cloudflare, or from GitHub if that server does not answer.
  • backup routing provider (Google) — backup routing and speed limitsReceives search text, origin, destination, location/course when rerouting and the map area around the route directly from the phone, under a random guest account per installation. It is used when you choose Backup or the primary route source fails; even with Google selected, the app may ask backup routing provider to add speed limits where the routes share a road.
  • Google speech recognition (optional)Only when you pick Google as the recogniser for voice replies: audio from the watch's microphone is sent to Google's speech recognition service on the phone to turn it into text, and Google may process it on its servers. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In, Google Play and FirebaseSign-in, purchases, integrity checks and administrator announcements. See the Google privacy policy and the Firebase privacy information.
  • A paid place indexA server-side fallback when Google returns no search result. The phone never contacts it directly.
  • OpenFreeMapOpenStreetMap data for the road map on Garmin and Amazfit watches.
  • Watch companion appsHuawei Health (Wear Engine), Zepp, Mi Fitness or Garmin Connect carry guidance, maps and caller names to the watch you paired. With voice replies on, Huawei Health passes speech from the watch to G Navigation and takes the text back.
  • Technical error reportsAfter you confirm a manual send, or separately opt into automatic call/map error reports (at most one every 6 hours per channel), a report is sent to the G Navigation server on Cloudflare to fix errors. It includes phone/Android, app and companion versions, watch metadata, permission/switch states, technical call/map/voice traces, structured process exit reasons, error classes and stack locations; raw logcat is excluded. It does not deliberately record audio or attach audio files; log lines can contain metadata arising during app use. The bundle is encrypted on the phone to the developer's key and the developer can decrypt it; it is not anonymous data. App version, phone model, installation ID and receipt time are sent separately as report metadata. Turning off automatic sending or withdrawing consent stops queued automatic reports too; it does not delete reports already sent. Server reports are retained for up to 90 days; contact the developer with the report ticket to request earlier deletion.
  • CloudflareRuns the G Navigation server: accounts, the trial, Premium, and forwarding searches and routes. The server keeps no search text or route coordinates.
05

Community road signs

Contributing road signs is optional, off by default, and requires your separate agreement to policy version 12. Navigation and reading accepted signs remain available without contributing. Initially, uploads require a signed-in account with a recently verified session; the AppGallery edition does not yet support reporting.

When you report a fixed camera, maximum speed sign or end of a speed restriction, the phone records the report location, GPS accuracy, heading, speed, vehicle, sign type and value, day, app version and random report ID. The queue stays in private app storage for up to 7 days and 200 entries. Uploads happen only after the trip ends, or when the app is idle, online and not navigating; nothing is uploaded during navigation.

Reports travel over HTTPS to the G Navigation server on Cloudflare. The sign-in session verifies permission and quotas; report tables use keyed account and device pseudonyms instead of the original account ID, with days rather than exact times. These records are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Network infrastructure still processes ordinary request information such as IP addresses to provide and secure the service.

Raw reports are deleted after 60 days. Daily value and weight aggregates with no reporter identifier last up to 180 days; reporter/day rejection counts last 90 days. Sign-specific deduplication tokens and vote weights contain neither raw report coordinates nor an identifier reusable across signs; they remain pseudonymous and last until the sign is deleted or you withdraw contributions. Retired and rejected signs are deleted after 180 days.

Turning off Community road signs clears the local queue, blocks further uploads and requests deletion of raw reports and reporter reputation when online. Account deletion also removes these records. Sign-specific tokens are removed in bounded batches and the associated voting weight is subtracted; re-enabling waits for this erasure to finish. A minimal ban record remains after contribution withdrawal to prevent evading moderation; deleting the account removes it. Accepted signs may remain publicly available as aggregate location, heading, type, value and totals without an account or reporter identifier. Already downloaded or cached public copies cannot be recalled immediately.

By submitting, you grant G Navigation a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, verify, edit, aggregate and distribute the road-sign content to provide and improve the service. This licence continues for aggregate signs that are no longer linked to you after the original report is deleted. Do not submit other people's personal information. Physical signs and actual road conditions always take priority; community data may be wrong or out of date.

06

Retention and deletion

  • On the phone: the current route, recent destinations and your saved places, until you delete them.
  • Temporary caller-name contacts are removed after a call ends. For Huawei watches, Zalo and Messenger contacts are kept so the name shows sooner next time and are deleted after 30 days without a call; you can choose to delete them after every call, or delete them all with the “Delete the contacts G Navigation made” button in the app.
  • Phone logs: the call-card log holds up to 300 events for 48 hours; the call-step log holds up to 1,200 lines, 256 KiB and 48 hours. Age pruning runs during app activity or log reads/writes; data may remain while the app is stopped. Disabling all call alerts or withdrawing consent clears call logs. Map and voice logs have their own line limits and keep the newest lines; clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Sent reports are retained on the server for up to 90 days; turning automatic sending off only stops future sends.
  • Voice replies: audio and text exist only in the app's memory while they are recognised and are then discarded (with Google picked, the audio sent to Google is handled under Google's policy); messages waiting for a reply from the music panel stay in memory for at most 10 minutes. Your text formatting choices and shorthand list stay on the phone until you change them or uninstall the app; a downloaded Vietnamese model stays on the phone until you uninstall the app.
  • Account, sign-in session and trial data: kept until you delete the account. A hashed device identifier, not linked to the account, is kept for up to three years to prevent a repeated trial.
  • Purchases: kept as long as needed to grant and restore Premium and meet transaction obligations.
  • Firebase keeps installation identifiers until they are deleted; removal from Google's systems can take up to 180 days.

Deleting your account. In the app, open Settings > Account > Delete account. If you cannot open the app, email support@gnavigation.men with the subject “G Navigation account deletion” and your sign-in email. Deletion does not reset trial eligibility: a new account on the same device does not get a second trial.

Your choices. You can decline optional permissions, withdraw consent in the app, turn off notifications in Android settings or uninstall the app. Declining optional permissions does not block navigation.

07

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Service keys live on the server, not in the app. Session tokens are stored hashed and the app turns off system backup. No transmission or storage is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, please contact us.
