Privacy Policy

G Viewer displays turn guidance, route maps and arrival information received from G Navigation on your paired phone. It provides location-based guidance without reading the watch GPS or creating a watch-side account.

Plain-language summary

After your consent, Huawei Wear Engine carries guidance, map images and route-position information from the paired phone to the watch. Location and guidance are processed in memory; map images are temporary app-private files. Consent, display and vibration preferences, and a basic device display profile are stored locally.

03

Data G Viewer processes

The phone supplies location-derived information, including map anchor and current-position coordinates, heading, route images, street names, distances and arrival information. This can reveal your location and journey context even though G Viewer does not request the watch GPS permission. This personal information is used to provide the navigation display.

G Viewer does not read the watch GPS, health data, contacts, call logs, microphone, camera, photos or personal files. It has no watch-side account, advertising identifier, advertising, analytics or generative AI service. It does process the location-related content received from the phone.

04

Device permissions and consent condition

The G Viewer HAP declares no reqPermissions system permission. It does not request location, health, sensor, contact, notification, microphone, camera, photo, or file access.

  • Before you select Agree, G Viewer displays the privacy notice only and does not register a Huawei Wear Engine P2P receiver.
  • After you agree, G Viewer stores the local consent marker and handshakes only with the configured package and certificate identity of com.gnavigation.mobile.
  • Selecting Cancel exits before the P2P channel is registered. Ending navigation or closing G Viewer stops guidance display.
05

Sharing and platform services

G Viewer does not send guidance to a developer-operated server, sell data, or share data with advertising networks or data brokers. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.

Guidance travels through Huawei Wear Engine between the paired phone and watch. Huawei operates that platform service under Huawei's own terms and policies; G Viewer uses it only as a P2P channel with authenticated G Navigation. G Viewer does not itself make HTTP requests or network connections to our servers.

G Viewer sends its basic device display profile (screen dimensions, shape, model and API version), app/protocol version and connection acknowledgements to G Navigation on the paired phone through Huawei Wear Engine. The phone uses this information to prepare compatible navigation content and manage the connection.

06

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can decline the initial notice before the P2P receiver is registered. To stop guidance, end navigation on the phone or close G Viewer. To remove consent, saved preferences, the device cache and any remaining temporary files, clear app data where supported or uninstall G Viewer. There is no separate in-app consent-withdrawal button. Phone-side data and permissions are managed separately in G Navigation.

G Viewer does not upload your journey to a developer-operated server. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, contact support@gnavigation.men; explain which app and device are involved. We can explain local deletion steps, but cannot retrieve watch-only data from a server.
