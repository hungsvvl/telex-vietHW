Privacy Policy

G Navigation for iPhone is motorcycle navigation on OpenStreetMap data that sends turn instructions to a paired Huawei watch. This page says which data leaves your device, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

In plain terms

The app uses your location for routes and guidance and sends map, search and routing requests to the relevant services. Apple or Google sign-in and road-sign contributions are optional. After contribution policy 15 consent, G Navigation receives the submitted sign points and any photo you choose, and may receive sign points from routes already loaded. Notifications are separately optional and off by default. The app uses no advertising, behavioural analytics or advertising identifier.

Data the app does not touch

The app does not access contacts, calendars, health data, microphone, browsing history or an advertising identifier. It does not read other apps’ notifications. The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a sign; the system photo picker supplies only the photo you select, without general photo-library access. A watch can receive turn instructions and optional map frames; contribution photos are not sent to the watch.

05A

Road-sign contributions · consent 15

This optional feature is off by default. Contribution consent 15 is separate from the general app privacy notice 4. The following is the same iOS-specific disclosure shown by the app before contribution consent:

Contributing road signs is optional and off by default. One explicit agreement to policy version 15 enables both manual contributions and automatic contributions of fixed cameras and road-speed attributes from routes actually loaded through the routing service. Both forms are enabled together after you agree. Previous agreement to version 14 does not replace this agreement. Navigation and reading accepted signs remain available without contributing. On iOS, uploads require a signed-in account with a recently Apple App Attest verified session.

When you report a sign or fixed camera, the phone records the report location, whether it comes from GPS or a point you choose on the map, applicable heading, vehicle, sign type and value, day, app version and random report ID. Reporting at your current location uses GPS accuracy and speed. When you drag the map to place a sign, only your chosen point is sent; GPS accuracy and speed are left empty, without also sending the device GPS location or full route. Map-selected points require administrator review and create no automatic GPS confirmation votes. Weight and dimension restrictions retain their original numeric value and tonnes or metres separately from speed. You may add a private description of up to 200 characters; for an unclassified sign it can replace a photo. Administrators read it to classify the sign; descriptions are never published. Descriptions are deleted with raw reports after 60 days, on contribution withdrawal or account deletion. The queue stays in private app storage for up to 7 days and 200 entries. Uploads happen only after the trip ends, or when the app is idle, online and not navigating; manual reports are not uploaded during navigation.

You can also contribute prohibitions, directions, areas and fixed road warnings while stationary, without starting navigation. The applicable direction is estimated automatically from the compass while you point the phone towards the sign; it is recorded when you take the photo, or when you tap Save contribution if there is no photo. The direction saved in the queue stays unchanged when the report is uploaded later. Photos are optional: only when you tap Take photo does the app request camera permission if needed and open the phone camera inside the app; you may skip photos. Images are re-encoded to remove EXIF and image location metadata, limited to 64 KiB, stored separately in private app storage and sent only with a report you confirm. Photograph the sign, avoiding faces and vehicle plates. Photos are private objects on Cloudflare R2; only the authenticated reporter and administrators can view them for verification, and photos never appear in public sign data. Deleting an unsent report removes only its local record and photo. Uploaded photos are cleaned up with raw reports after 60 days, when you withdraw contributions or delete the account. Server deletion requires a connection.

Reports travel over HTTPS to the G Navigation server on Cloudflare. The sign-in session verifies permission and quotas; report tables use keyed account and device pseudonyms instead of the original account ID, with days rather than exact times. These records are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Network infrastructure still processes ordinary request information such as IP addresses to provide and secure the service. When an administrator requests a road or place name for a sign, the server sends the sign coordinates to the configured Nominatim service. The service receives the coordinates and the server connection, not photos, account identifiers, routes or GPS traces. Derived names are cached for up to 30 days to reduce lookups; names do not change sign locations or grouping.

Raw reports are deleted after 60 days. Daily value and weight aggregates with no reporter identifier last up to 180 days; reporter/day rejection counts last 90 days. Sign-specific deduplication tokens and vote weights contain neither raw report coordinates nor an identifier reusable across signs; they remain pseudonymous and last until the sign is deleted or you withdraw contributions. Retired and rejected signs are deleted after 180 days.

Turning off Community road signs clears the local queue, blocks further uploads and requests deletion of raw reports and reporter reputation when online. Account deletion also removes these records. Sign-specific tokens are removed in bounded batches and the associated voting weight is subtracted; re-enabling waits for this erasure to finish. A minimal ban record remains after contribution withdrawal to prevent evading moderation; deleting the account removes it. Accepted signs may remain publicly available as aggregate location, heading, type, value and totals without an account or reporter identifier. Already downloaded or cached public copies cannot be recalled immediately.

By submitting, you grant G Navigation a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, verify, edit, aggregate and distribute the road-sign content to provide and improve the service. This licence continues for aggregate signs that are no longer linked to you after the original report is deleted. Do not submit other people's personal information. Physical signs and actual road conditions always take priority; community data may be wrong or out of date.

When enabled, the app takes fixed-camera data and road-speed attributes from routes actually loaded through the routing service. A road-speed attribute is not evidence that a physical speed sign was seen. Only the observation type, sign type and value, vehicle, location, heading and app version are sent over HTTPS to the G Navigation server on Cloudflare; no full route, destination, GPS trace or photo is sent. Automatic contributions may upload while moving, loading a route or rerouting. Reports you create manually are still uploaded only outside navigation.

The separate local queue holds up to 200 points for 7 days; duplicate suppression keeps up to 2,000 hashes for 7 days. Turning off Community road signs or switching accounts clears this queue. The server uses keyed account pseudonyms to verify consent and quotas: consent records are removed after 7 idle days and quota counters are retained for 2 days. These are pseudonymous records used to control uploads.

Provider candidates have their locations rounded to about 1 m and headings to 15°, contain no reporter identifier and create no confirmation votes. They are shared records kept for up to 90 days since the latest provider observation, including after moderation. Turning off Community road signs or deleting the account stops new uploads, clears the separate local queue and revokes server consent when online; shared candidates are not linked to the sender and remain until retention expires, so they cannot be withdrawn per account. Provider data may be wrong or out of date; physical signs and actual conditions always take priority.

Apple or Google sign-in is optional. The server receives the provider user ID, name and verified email when available, and a hashed installation ID to issue a session. Apple and Google passwords are never stored. Apple authorization grants are encrypted for revocation when you delete your account. App Attest public keys and receipts verify sessions and are deleted with the account. Delete the account from Contributions or Settings → Account; App Store purchases remain managed separately by Apple.

OpenFreeMap supplies map styles, vector tiles based on OpenStreetMap and font glyphs for the phone's Gnavi/OSM map and map frames sent to watches. The phone connects directly over HTTPS; the service receives its IP address and requested map tiles, which can reveal the viewed map area. Glyph requests include the font stack and character range. These requests do not include a G Navigation account, destination, search text or contribution photo.

Retention and deletion

Recent searches, favourites and settings stay on this iPhone. Restorable routes expire after one day; private unsent contribution queues and photos last at most 7 days. Use the app to clear local history and saved places. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Contribution server retention is stated in the contribution 15 section: raw reports and photos at most 60 days, separately retained aggregate support, and accepted public signs without a public reporter identity. Turning contributions off withdraws consent, clears the private queue and requests deletion of raw reports and reputation data when online. Public accepted signs and already downloaded public copies can remain as explained there.

Delete the server account from Contributions or Settings → Account to delete its account and App Attest records and revoke Apple authorization. Deleting the app alone does not delete the server account. Turning notifications off requests deletion of the separate push registration, with retry after a network failure. For access or deletion requests, use the contact below.

08

Choices and withdrawing consent

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services: change or revoke location access, and turn Precise Location off.
  • Huawei Health: revoke the Wear Engine access granted to G Navigation.
  • In the app: disconnect the watch, clear the place history, unstar favourites.
  • Refusing location does not stop the app from opening; features that use your current position and follow a journey require the corresponding location access.
  • Contributions: withdraw contribution consent and clear the private queue, or delete the account. Navigation and reading accepted signs remain available without contributing.
  • Camera and photos: omit a sign photo, or revoke camera permission in iOS Settings. Only an image you choose is attached.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app and revoke notification permission in iOS Settings. A server-registration deletion retries when the app opens if the network was unavailable.
09

Children and security

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children’s data. Optional signed-in contributions contain user-submitted sign information and selected photos; do not submit information or images about other people.

External network requests use HTTPS. Huawei Wear Engine protects its Bluetooth transport. The optional Amazfit endpoint is restricted to 127.0.0.1 on this iPhone, and Zepp relays to the watch over Bluetooth. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; contact us about a security or privacy problem.
